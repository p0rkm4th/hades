# Household authority acceptance

Detailed authenticated household dogfood uses private deployment identities
and is retained in the private `hades-infra` archive at
`docs/private-public-hades-source-archive-20261001/`.

Public tests use synthetic Alpha/Beta/Gamma identities and verify isolation,
revocation, owner-only grants, and fail-closed behavior. They do not establish
real household acceptance or disclose production identities.
